These icons started as Pixtober, my series of small pixel icons drawn one a day on a 16×16 grid: in colour for Pixtober 2024, then in black and white for Pixtober 2025. Along the way the style settled into a few quiet rules: a closed outline, flat colours, no stray pixels, and one clear idea you can read at a glance.

This site is those rules written down, so your AI tool can draw in the same style for any word you give it. The icons in the library are free to use in anything you make. If you make something with them, I’d love to see it.
